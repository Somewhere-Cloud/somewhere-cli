## Public wording

Before changing anything a person or an outside agent will read (an email, a page, a screen, docs, CLI or connector text, or an error message), read the public wording guide on the board with `tasks get tsk_f7d14f1e6d2448a89ec24c08cd488248 -p default`, then read the guide for your surface under it.

Use the strictest category that applies:

1. In the founder’s name or quoting him. He sees the exact text before it goes out.
2. Communication sent to people. Ship under the communication policy and report the before and after so he is told.
3. Public consumer-facing pages and screens.
4. Agent-facing docs and tool text.

Keep our internal state, plans and pending applications out of public wording. Never present a gap in our own information as a gap in the product. The full policy is on the board.
